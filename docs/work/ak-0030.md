## Scope

Read BMFont XML, measure text and compose tinted glyphs without Skia.

## Criteria

- `readBitmapFont(xml)` reads one unpacked RGBA page, including either `glyphAtlas` layout, and
  validates counts, code points, metrics and rectangles.
- `measureBitmapText` and `drawBitmapText` require explicit `lineSpacing` and `missing` policy and
  keep literal LF lines, signed metrics and original-code kerning.
- `drawBitmapText` lays out the text before mutating the target and tints glyph coverage through the
  straight-alpha compositor.

## Decisions

- Glyph bounds are declared rectangles, not a scan of visible pixels.
- Alpha runs stay local to the games.
