## Scope

Write the Aseprite 1.2.30 sheet PNG and json-hash data without an Aseprite executable.

## Criteria

- `spriteSheet(options, tags)` returns the PNG bytes and json-hash text the Aseprite export writes,
  byte for byte; `writeSpriteSheet` writes them.
- The PNG reproduces libpng 1.6.38 filter choice and IDAT chunking and the Chromium zlib deflate
  inside Aseprite 1.2.30, ported to TypeScript.
- Frozen Aseprite exports in `node/tests/fixtures/sprite-sheet` prove the format.

## Decisions

- The encoder is ported rather than wrapping Aseprite, so builds need no Aseprite.

## Outcome

Shipped in 0.16.0 (9c0e5c8).
