## Scope

The three functions approved from the [shared raster and colour
proposal](../shared-raster-colour-proposal.md).

## Criteria

- `resizeLanczos3` filters premultiplied Float32 horizontally then vertically and renormalizes
  support at image edges.
- `gaussianBlur` takes a positive sigma, uses support `ceil(3*sigma)` with repeated edges and
  filters the full image.
- `colour.oklabToSrgb({ gamut, rounding }, lab)` requires explicit gamut and rounding policies.
- Frozen synthetic references cover the filters and inverse Oklab.

## Decisions

- Gamut and rounding are required options, not defaults.
- Contour, blend and 3D helpers stay out of scope.

## Outcome

Shipped in 0.23.0 (ef7a94e, 1273142).
