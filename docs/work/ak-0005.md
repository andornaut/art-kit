## Scope

Animated GIF and ICO encoding with Pillow 13.0.0 references.

## Criteria

- `encodeGif` writes whole frames with exact global or local palettes and a transparency index for
  binary alpha, merging identical consecutive frames as Pillow does.
- `encodeIco` writes one 32-bit PNG entry per pre-sized image.
- Fixtures record Pillow's decodes of its own saves, and a cross-check decodes the Node output with
  Pillow.

## Decisions

- GIF, ICO and palette reduction share the raster types and container policy (c3d3621).

## Outcome

Shipped in 0.12.0 (9feafef).
