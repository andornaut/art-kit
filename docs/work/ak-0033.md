## Scope

A development diagnostic that compares two decoded RGBA images.

## Criteria

- `compareRgba({ backgrounds }, expected, actual)` reports a dimension mismatch, or raw equality and
  channel errors including RGB under zero alpha.
- Each opaque background adds composite RGB RMSE and maximum error.
- No decoding, file access, colour-profile normalization or acceptance decision.

## Decisions

- It ships under `build/`, not as a runtime or test-only API.
- Thresholds, budgets and visual acceptance stay with callers.

## Outcome

Shipped in 0.27.0 (d45b5dc).
