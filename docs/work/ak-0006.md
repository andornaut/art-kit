## Scope

`quantise` reduces frames to one shared palette of at most `maxColours` entries.

## Criteria

- Median cut over opaque colours weighted by pixel count, rounded weighted means and nearest-entry
  mapping, with fixed tie order and integer arithmetic.
- Fully transparent pixels keep one entry; frames that already fit return unchanged.

## Decisions

- Integer arithmetic and fixed tie order make the palette deterministic.

## Outcome

Shipped in 0.12.0 (9c002db). The `quantise` subpath was removed in 0.22.0 ([ak-0023](ak-0023.md)).
