## Scope

Breaking 0.18.0 cleanup of the frame types, `grid`, `raster` and `rgbaImage`.

## Criteria

- `Rgb`, `ColourContext`, `Tag` and `Sprite` are defined once in the Skia-free raster module, and
  one colour resolver serves `frameToRgba`.
- `grid` exports `opaqueRows`, `opaqueCols` and `flipped`; edge rules are nullable and hex colours
  are validated whole.
- `raster.composeSheet`, `frameToRgba` and `composeContactSheet` replace the Canvas `frames` and
  `generate` subpaths; frozen digests pin the contact sheets.
- `blitExtruded` takes options first and the target last.

## Decisions

- Only `fonts` needs Skia.

## Outcome

Shipped in 0.18.0 (a34a1da, 9e531e1).
