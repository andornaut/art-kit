## Scope

Separate glyph rendering in `fonts` from atlas packing in `glyphAtlas`.

## Criteria

- `fonts.rasteriseGlyphs` returns each glyph drawn alone and trimmed to its coverage, with the line
  metrics and optional all-pair kerning.
- The second packer and XML writer leave `fonts`; callers pack with `glyphAtlas`.
- Frozen fixtures from 0.16.0 pin both games' font pipelines byte for byte.

## Decisions

- Callers apply glyph policy between rendering and packing.

## Outcome

Shipped in 0.18.0 (d22f452).
