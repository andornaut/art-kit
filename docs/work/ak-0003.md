## Scope

Pillow 13.0.0-equivalent RGBA operations on buffers, without a canvas.

## Criteria

- Fill, masked and RGB-only paste and alpha compositing reproduce Pillow integer maths, including
  partial alpha.
- Crop, flip, scale, resize, rotation, channel, grayscale, alpha and lookup-table operations return
  new images.
- `composeContactSheet` builds first-frame or all-frames preview sheets.
- Committed Pillow reference fixtures prove byte equality.

## Decisions

- Options-first `fill`, `paste` and `composite` replace the Pillow-named functions, keeping every
  fixture output byte for byte (8534374).

## Outcome

Shipped in 0.11.0 (4a05739, 8534374).
