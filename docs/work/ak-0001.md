## Scope

One container policy for checks, packaging, provisioning and wheel verification.

## Criteria

- Every container runs as uid 19001 with `HOME=/tmp`, a read-only root, dropped capabilities and
  four CPUs.
- Host paths are read-only bind mounts; writable state is a per-run named volume at `/tmp`, and
  results leave through `docker cp`.
- CI calls the same scripts.

## Decisions

- Committed trees arrive on stdin, so a check reads the commit rather than the working copy.
- The container and its volume are removed on exit.

## Outcome

Shipped in 0.11.0 (f2db49a).
