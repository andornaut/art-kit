## Scope

Cut redundant test cases while keeping realistic and critical coverage.

## Criteria

- About 40% fewer logical cases, with any coverage loss explicit and low risk.
- No application, API, dependency or configuration changes.

## Decisions

- Runtime is measured separately from the case count.

## Outcome

Shipped in 0.24.0 (51b9576): 12329 to 7228 logical cases, 41.4% fewer.
